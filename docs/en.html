A management decision team, inside Claude

You have a business decision. Nine roles sit at the table, run the meeting, stop halfway to ask you what only you know, and hand back a decision with owners and dates. For any business, from a bakery to a factory.

One promise: it never invents a number. Every figure says where it came from.

This is how they talk

Farid (Spotter)Hold on. At the start you said you send boxes to two offices on Thursdays. So you already have a delivery route and corporate customers, and nobody has counted them?
Marta (Analyst)I worked out the second branch. Rent 28, four salaries 96, energy 15. That is 139M a month assumed. It needs 46 orders a day. The branch you have does 129 observed. Daily orders in the new neighbourhood: no data.
Sheila (Coordinator)Stopping the meeting here. You know this and we are guessing. When the queue forms at six, roughly how many people leave without buying?
Bruno (Critic)That 42 percent margin is last year's flour. At 35 the break-even goes to 55 a day. The number this whole decision sits on is itself an assumption.

Three steps to your first meeting

Have a Claude account. Shora runs inside Claude and is free itself. Get the Claude Code app from claude.com. If you only use claude.ai, see the questions below.

Type these two lines in Claude Code.

/plugin marketplace add mahdifooladvand/shora
/plugin install shora@shora

Say the topic. For example: "hold a meeting about whether we open a second branch". It asks three short questions, starts the meeting, and stops halfway for the numbers only you have.

What it refuses to do

Invent a number. Market size, growth rate, a competitor's revenue: if you did not supply it, it writes "no data" and says how to find out.
Pass a guess off as a measurement. Every figure carries one of three tags: observed, assumed, no data. Arithmetic does not turn an assumption into an observation.
Design secret tests on your staff. A check you could not read aloud to the team is a trap.
Give legal, tax or medical advice. It tells you who to ask.

Questions

I use claude.ai, not Claude Code.

Download the skill folder from GitHub and upload its contents as a skill, or paste it into a Project's instructions. The full guide is on the GitHub page. The mid-meeting stop and the manager page work there too; only the automatic file save does not.

Where does my company's information go?

Shora is a text file that tells Claude how to run a meeting. It has no server, no account and no service of its own. What you type goes where every other Claude conversation goes, and the manager page is saved on your own computer.

Does it work in my language?

Yes. It answers in whatever language you write. Persian has its own guide here.

What does it cost?

Shora is free and open source under the MIT licence. Claude's own subscription is yours.

I found a problem, or I have an idea.

Open an issue on the GitHub page. The most useful thing to add is a note for your industry: what a controller in your field actually inspects.
